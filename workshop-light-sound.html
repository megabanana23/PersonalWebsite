Let's Light Things Up and Make Sound!

A safe, hands-on introduction to electricity and engineering using snap-together components to build working circuits.

Workshop overview

Engineering sound and light.

Designed for students in grades 2–6, this tool-free session introduced electricity and engineering. Students built working circuits and integrated components such as speakers and fans.

Students explored

  • Building working snap-together circuits
  • Connecting speakers, fans, and other components
  • How electricity powers everyday technology

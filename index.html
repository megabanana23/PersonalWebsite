The Real Temp

The Real Temp turns a complicated weather report into a clear picture of what it actually feels like outside—and what matters next. I founded, designed, and developed the product from its first idea through its live web experience.

Explore the live app ↗
Inside the product +

How it works

  1. Choose a place.Search by city or ZIP code, use your current location, or open a saved Home, Work, or pinned location.
  2. See the essentials first.The dashboard leads with the feels-like temperature, conditions, and forecast so the answer is easy to understand at a glance.
  3. Go deeper when needed.Open individual tiles for hourly and five-day forecasts, humidity, wind, air quality, UV, pollen, and weather-map details.

Built around the user

  • Practical outdoor and clothing guidance
  • Customizable, rearrangeable dashboard tiles
  • Temperature and wind-unit preferences
  • Light, dark, and system appearance options
